Coop Drawing

Draw on the same canvas as someone else's Krita in real time, in the spirit of Drawpile.

Getting started

  1. Open the docker: Settings > Dockers > Coop Drawing.
  2. Host: open the document you want to share, type your name, and press Start hosting. Press Copy invite and send the address and code to your partner. The first time, Windows may ask whether Krita may accept network connections. Allow it for private networks.
  3. Join: on the other computer, open the Join tab, enter the address and code, and press Join. A new document opens with the host's layers.

Everyone needs the same version of the co-op build of Krita; a partner whose version speaks another protocol is refused.

Drawing together

  • Every layer is shared. Everyone can draw on, erase, fill, transform, lock, rename, reorder and delete any layer, including the same layer at the same time.
  • When two people paint the very same pixels at the same moment, the change that reaches the host last wins for those pixels. Everything else from both strokes is kept.
  • Strokes are shared while they are painted, and your partner's paint appears while you are drawing. Three things wait until you finish what you are doing: paint under your own stroke in progress, changes to a layer you have open in Transform, Move or a filter dialog, and layers being added, deleted or moved.
  • Instant Preview is switched off during a session and switched back on afterwards.
  • Hiding a layer is personal: it only hides it for you.

Undo

In the shared document, Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac), Edit > Undo and the toolbar button undo your own last change, for everyone. Your partner's work is never undone, and paint they put on top of yours stays. It doesn't matter who undoes first: if everyone undoes everything, the canvas is back to how it started. Redo works the same way. The docker also has Undo mine and Redo mine buttons, plus a count of your undo steps.

  • What Ctrl+Z skips: layer changes (add, delete, move, rename, opacity...), selections, and edits on layers that aren't shared. Ctrl+Z stops there with a message; press it again to undo your painting before them. An action that also deleted layers, such as Merge Down, can't be undone during a session. Deleting a shared layer is final for everyone.
  • Not undoable: what was on the canvas when the session started, and a new layer's first content.
  • Tools still open: with Transform, Move or a filter dialog still open, finish or cancel it before pressing Ctrl+Z.
  • Limits: 100 steps per person. Resizing the canvas or switching animation frames clears your undo history. The Undo History docker is greyed out for the shared document.
  • After the session: undo works for what you do from then on, but not for steps from the session. Save and reopen the document to get a clean history.

Good to know

  • Only paint layers and groups are shared. Vector, fill, filter, file and clone layers, masks, layer styles, selections and animation frames other than the current one stay local.
  • The host controls the canvas size.
  • Connections are protected by the session code but are not encrypted. Use a network you trust, or a VPN such as Tailscale or ZeroTier. That is also the easiest way to draw together over the internet.
